# 0011 — Routing never committed: truncated queue IDs

## Symptom
Route_Case_To_Queue reached updDefault in the debug trace and reported success,
but Owner, Routing_Decision__c and Routed_Timestamp__c stayed blank on every Case.
Removing the start condition and deleting the decision element changed nothing.

## Diagnosis
An Execute Anonymous update threw: System.StringException: Invalid id: 3D00GjV00000354vh

The eight Routing_Map__mdt rows held 17-character queue IDs. Salesforce IDs are
15 or 18 characters. They had been copied out of Lightning URLs, which render the
ID with a 3D0 prefix and drop the UAA suffix.

## Contributing factor
Neither updFound nor updDefault had a fault connector wired to updFault, so the DML
exception was swallowed and the element appeared to succeed. This is why three
separate diagnostics pointed at the wrong thing.

## Fix
Queried Group WHERE Type='Queue' for authoritative 18-character IDs. Two rows were
also aimed at the wrong queue (ASSAULT pointed at Campus Emergency Dispatch and FIRE
at Duress, reversed). All eight rows corrected.

## Verification
Case 00001037 routed FIRE / MB-03 to Campus Emergency Dispatch with a timestamp.

## What it costs
- Roughly two hours lost to the start condition, the decision element and the field
  rows, none of which were at fault.
- Lesson: every DML element gets a fault connector on day one. A silent no-op is far
  more expensive to debug than a loud error.
- Lesson: never hand-type or URL-copy an ID. Query it.
- Outstanding: the start condition guard (Routed_Timestamp__c Is Null) still needs to
  go back on; the flow currently re-routes on every save.
